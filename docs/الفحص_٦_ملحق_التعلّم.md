# «أنشودتي»: review of the learning and assessment design (review 6)

Reviewer stance: an adversarial learning scientist seeing the app for the first time. I did not change the repo. I ran `python3 build.py` (built version 2026-11-10, 39 poems), served `www/` on port 8773 and drove the real code (`startQuiz`, `nextQ`, `stampEffects`, `finishQuiz`, `segments`) with Playwright.
Scripts and raw data are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/c9ec53c8-40a6-5db4-b7fa-8602a64d4718/scratchpad/review6/learn/`: `extract.py` → `quiz.json` (18,384 generated quiz instances), `analyse.py` → `guess.txt`, `meaning_pass.py`, `probe.py`, `probe2.py`, `load.py`.

**Scores**
- **Learning design: 6.5/10.** The memorisation core is better than most commercial apps: vanishing cues, three-round echo with fading for the youngest, recall judged by a human, expanding spacing, and stumbled verses recorded per verse. Three faults pull it down. The progression spine ignores recall for two of the three children. Echo mastery for the 6-year-old never enters spaced review. Massed re-recordings are counted as spaced reviews.
- **Assessment: 4/10.** The only valid measure of memorisation is the parent stamp (🧠), and it is a good one. ⭐ is the gate for the path, and it measures recognition. A child who never reads the poem can get it by random tapping, it can be assembled from two different pieces of a long poem, and once earned it is never lost. The «ما معنى البيت؟» items are well built (surface strategies stay at chance level). The «تدريب» rules are sound in intent but leak through the child-selectable easy mode.

Each finding is tagged as one of:
- **[FIX]**: a pure fix with no pedagogical choice in it.
- **[PARENT]**: changes what counts as success or how much work the family does, so the parent should decide.

## Measurements

### M1. Guessability per quiz type
From `guess.txt`. Each strategy uses only surface cues. "Passed" means ≥80% of the counted questions, which is the rule in `finishQuiz`.

| Quiz (mode) | Counts for ⭐? | Strategy without knowing the poem | Per question | Counted quizzes passed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| أكمل البيت (normal), age 10 | 100% | random | 33% | 3.9% |
| 〃 | | **«familiar»: picks the ending that belongs to this poem (a child who has only *read* it)** | **60%** | **38%** |
| 〃 | | familiar + length closest to the first hemistich | 64% | **44%** |
| 〃 | | length match alone | 45% | 13% |
| 〃 | | rhyme of the poem | 31% | 3.5% |
| أكمل البيت (normal), ages 8 and 6 | 91–95% | familiar + length | 64–65% | 32–34% |
| أكمل البيت (easy) | 0% (practice) | familiar | **100%** | n/a |
| ما معنى الكلمة؟ (text items) | 77–98% | **shares root letters with the word** | **48–50%** | 13–16% of quizzes |
| 〃 (easy, pictures answered from general vocabulary at p=0.9) | | root letters + vocabulary | | 21–32% |
| ما معنى البيت؟ | ~100% | longest / shortest / word overlap | 33–38% | 2–6% (**good**) |
| رتّب الأبيات (easy, 3 verses, child-selectable at any age) | 92% at age 10 | random tapping that remembers rejected options | 50% per window | **12.5%** (3 windows, all needed) |
| رتّب الأبيات (normal, 4 verses) | | 〃 | 17% per window | 0.5% |

- **A third of «أكمل البيت» questions (33–37%) have both wrong options taken from other poems.** These are the paired-rhyme fables: the poem's own lines do not share the right rhyme, so `others` (other poems with the same rhyme) fill the distractors. Recognising which line belongs to this poem is then enough; the child does not need to know which ending goes with which beginning.
- The order windows inside one quiz overlap by 28–50% (mean pairwise overlap of `orderStarts` windows). After the first window, the second is largely given away.

### M2. Time to ⭐ for a 10-year-old who does not know the poem
Monte Carlo over the per-day pass rates above. One counted attempt per type per day, which is the `qday` rule.

| Child's state | By day 3 | By day 7 | By day 14 | By day 30 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Never read the poem, taps at random (complete, meaning, sense, order-easy each day) | n/a | 44% | **78%** | 98% |
| Has read the poem once, cannot pair the hemistichs | 58% | **91%** | 99% | n/a |

⭐ completes a path step (`stepDone`), so the path can be walked this way.

### M3. ⭐ assembled from two different pieces
`probe2.py`, age 8, «الأسد والثعلب والعجل» (24 verses, 6 pieces):
- «ما معنى الكلمة؟» passed on piece 1 (verses 1–4).
- «أكمل البيت» passed on piece 2 (verses 5–8).
- Result: `qz={meaning:100, complete:100}`, `star=1`, `stepDone=true`.

The child never met 16 of the 24 verses. Both `s.qz` and `s.qday` are stored per poem, not per piece.

### M4. Massed re-recordings advance the spacing schedule
`probe.py`. «✓ حفظه» on piece 0 gives step 1, due in 3 days. Four review takes were then recorded *the same day*, each stamped «✓ ثابت»:

`step 2 (7 d) → step 3 (21 d) → step 4 (60 d) → 60 d`

`isReview = memOk && mem.includes(recSeg)` (template.html:818) does not check `reviewsDue`, so any level-4 take of a memorised piece is a "review", even one recorded on the day it was memorised.

### M5. Review streams and daily load
`load.py`. It mirrors `segments()`, `scheduleReview()` and «الكل» as an extra stream, with 15% «ضعُف».

| Child | Review streams on the path | Reviews per day after day 60 (mean / max) | Verses recited per day for review (mean / max) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Girl 10 | 58 | 2.1 / 7 | 19 / 77 |
| Boy 8 | 55 | 1.9 / 7 | 11 / 55 |
| Boy 6 | 72 | 2.5 / 7 | 11 / 43 |

- Parent listening time for reviews alone, three children, a new piece every 2 days: mean 4.8 min/day, max 15.3 min/day, 18 days over 10 min.
- The 6-year-old's echo sessions come on top of this. Each 3-verse session is ≈2 min, mostly the model's voice, and needs 70% heard before the stamp wakes.
- The load is manageable. It is not the problem. The problem is what happens when stamping stops (H3).

The 24-verse poem at age 6 has 8 pieces and therefore 9 review streams: 8 pieces plus «الكل», all reviewed separately once the whole is stamped.

### M6. Reading load for the non-reader (age 6, easy mode)
- 78% of «ما معنى الكلمة؟» questions are text items: 3,823 text against 1,079 picture items in 1,158 easy instances. 93% of easy meaning quizzes contain at least one text item that counts.
- Picture items also need reading: the prompt is the written hemistich plus «أيّ صورة هي «X»؟», with no audio.
- «أكمل البيت», «رتّب», and «ما معنى البيت؟» are all text-only.
- The only quiz that needs no reading is «اسمع واختر», and it exists only after the parent has recorded a model marked verse by verse.

## Findings, ranked

### CRITICAL

**C1. The main progression (path «ابدأ من هنا») is not tied to memorisation for the 8- and 10-year-olds.** [PARENT]
- *Evidence:*
  - `stepDone` is true when `b.quiz || b.mem` (template.html:579). For age >7, ⭐ is enough.
  - ⭐ is recognition only: two quiz types at ≥80%.
  - M2: 78% of children reach ⭐ in two weeks without knowing the poem. 91% do within a week after only reading it.
  - The path hint tells the child: «أنشِده، ثم انجح في اختبارين ⭐ لتنتقل». «أنشده» is not checked; any level-1 reading take would satisfy it anyway.
- *Why it matters:* the stated goal is memorisation. The thing the child is pushed to chase rewards recognition, and recognition MCQs overestimate recall badly. Children optimise the visible gate.
- *Fix (decision):* make the path step require 🧠 (parent-stamped recall of the whole poem), with ⭐ optional or a secondary dot. A softer alternative is ⭐ **and** a parent-stamped level-4 take of at least one piece. Either way, the paths.json `_note` (“⭐ أو 🧠”) must change; that is a parent decision.

**C2. ⭐ can be earned without knowing the poem: random tapping is enough, and the child can choose easy mode.** [FIX + PARENT]
- *Evidence:*
  - M1 and M2.
  - Easy order counts at every age: `typeCounts('order')===true` whatever `qEasy` is, and the 🐣 chip is available to the 10-year-old. A 3-verse window solved with ≤1 miss is a 50% coin flip under random tapping that remembers rejected options.
  - In «أكمل البيت» normal, 33–37% of items are solvable by familiarity alone.
- *Fixes:*
  - [FIX] Easy order should be practice for age >8, or require 0 misses (random pass falls from 50% to 17% per window).
  - [FIX] In «أكمل البيت», always include at least one distractor from **this** poem. For paired-rhyme poems, prefer same-poem endings over same-rhyme foreign ones, since the test is pairing, not rhyme.
  - [FIX] Better still, build "near-miss" distractors: the true hemistich with one word swapped for another word of the same poem. That tests verbatim recall, which is what memorisation needs.
  - [FIX] Draw order windows without overlap (`s+=n`) so each window is independent.
  - [PARENT] Raise the bar: ⭐ needs three types, or the same type passed on two different days (a spaced pass). One pass at ≥80% on 3–5 MCQ items is weak evidence.

### HIGH

**H1. ⭐ is per poem, not per piece. It can be built from two different pieces and is never lost.** [FIX]
- *Evidence:*
  - M3: `s.qz[type]` and `s.qday[type]` are keyed by poem.
  - `s.qz[type]=Math.max(...)` and `s.star=1` are never decayed.
- *Fix:* store `qz` per piece (or require that the pieces passed cover the poem), or let ⭐ count only from «الكل» for multi-piece poems.
- *Fix [PARENT]:* should a ⭐ expire if the poem's reviews keep failing?

**H2. Massed re-recordings are counted as spaced reviews and inflate the interval.** [FIX]
- *Evidence:* M4. Four same-day «ثابت» stamps take a piece from 3 days to 60 days. A child who proudly records the same piece several times in one afternoon, then a parent who stamps each take «ثابت», defeats spacing completely.
- *Fix:* a take is `review` only when the piece is due (`reviewsDue(...).includes(recSeg)`). Otherwise it is practice (`kind 'rec'`). Also, in `stampEffects`, advance the step at most once per due date: ignore `rok` when `rev[g].due > now`.

**H3. If the parent does not stamp, review stops silently.** [FIX + PARENT]
- *Evidence:*
  - A review take puts the piece in `rpend`. `reviewsDue` then excludes it, and `badges().due` is false.
  - `probe.py` "UNSTAMPED REVIEW": a piece 30 days overdue with an unstamped take shows **no** due marker anywhere for the child.
  - New takes wait in `pend` with no review scheduled.
  - After a week without stamping, the child sees no 🔁, the 6-year-old's path is frozen (it needs `eok`), and the queue holds roughly 7 days × 3 children × (≈2 reviews + new takes + echo sessions). The queue shows 8 items plus a count.
  - Nothing tells the parent the queue is ageing.
- *Fixes:*
  - [FIX] After N days (say 3), an unstamped review take should be treated as "attempted": schedule the same step again, so the child keeps retrieving.
  - [FIX] Show the age of the oldest waiting take on the parent page and in «مهمة الليلة».
  - [PARENT] Optionally allow the 10-year-old to self-rate a review («ثابت/تعثّرت») when the parent has not stamped within 48 h, with a parent spot-check later. Self-assessment of recall is reasonably accurate in 10-year-olds and much better than no review.

**H4. The 6-year-old's path advances on echo mastery that never enters spaced review.** [FIX + PARENT]
- *Evidence:*
  - For age ≤7, `stepDone` is true when every piece has `echoOk` (template.html:581).
  - `stampEffects('eok')` only adds to `echoOk`. It schedules **no** review (template.html:442).
  - Echo round 3 («البيت كله وحدك») is retrieval with a lag of about a minute, followed by immediate model feedback. That is good, but it is the only retrieval.
  - The child can walk the whole young path (17+ poems) with zero delayed retrieval. Forgetting at this age is steep; by the time the path is done, the first poems will be gone.
- *Fixes:*
  - [FIX] `eok` should schedule an echo-review at 1, 3 and 7 days. That review can be the same three-round echo, which needs no reading, or round 3 only.
  - [PARENT] Should the young path require a parent-stamped independent take (🎤 at level 4) before moving on?

**H5. Every quiz that can count needs reading. The 6-year-old non-reader cannot be assessed except by the parent.** [FIX]
- *Evidence:* M6. Even picture items present the target word in writing only.
- *Fix:* add a 🔊 to every picture item. Play the target word from the parent's verse-marked model (the `halves()` spans already exist), or a recorded word. Until then, keep quizzes out of the ≤7 flow entirely; the app partly does this, since the path uses echo.
- Also [FIX]: at age ≤7 the quiz menu should show only «اسمع واختر» and picture items with audio.

**H6. "Comprehension" is mostly recognition of text the child has just read.** [PARENT]
- *Evidence:*
  - «ما معنى البيت؟» items are well built: 27 poems have their own authored wrong meanings, and surface strategies stay at 33–38% per item. However, the right option is the same sentence shown under the verse in «معنى البيت» (`bmOn`, on by default from age 9). The child can match a memorised paraphrase instead of understanding the verse.
  - «ما معنى الكلمة؟» text items are 48–50% solvable by root-letter overlap. That is a known MCQ flaw: definitions reuse the root.
  - Pack lessons («الدرس كاملاً») are read-only, with answers one tap away in `<details>`. Nothing records whether they were discussed.
  - «حديث مع والدك» questions are the best comprehension element (open, elaborative, parent-mediated). They are untracked, and the README says they are a draft awaiting review.
- *Fixes:*
  - [FIX] Write meaning options that avoid the target's root.
  - [FIX] Hide «معنى البيت» for the rest of the day once a sense quiz has started that day, or rephrase the quiz option so it differs from the displayed paraphrase.
  - [PARENT] Add one transfer question per poem («لماذا…؟», «ماذا كنت ستفعل…؟») that the child answers by voice, so the parent can hear it in the same queue.

### MEDIUM

**M-a. Stumbled verses (`weak`) feed only «أكمل البيت» ordering.** [FIX]
- *Evidence:* template.html:1279. For ages ≤8, «أكمل البيت» defaults to easy, which is practice, so the best data the app collects (the parent's ear) barely changes what the child practises.
- *Fix:*
  - When a piece with weak verses is reviewed or sung, open at level 2 or 3 *for those verses only*.
  - Offer «تمرّن على البيت ٣» (echo of that verse plus the verse before it, for linking).
  - Schedule the next review of a piece with weak verses one step shorter.

**M-b. The first review comes after 3 days on «✓ حفظه».** [PARENT]
- *Evidence:* `scheduleReview(s,seg,1)`, with `REVIEW_DAYS=[1,3,7,21,60]`. For 6–8-year-olds, retrieval the next day after initial learning is the norm in memorisation programmes (and in hifz practice: sabaq → sabqi). A 3-day gap after a single same-day success risks forgetting enough for the first review to fail, which resets to 1 day. Intervals never go past 60 days, which is fine for a family programme.
- *Suggestion:* first review at 1 day for ages ≤8, whatever the stamp.

**M-c. Pieces and «الكل» are reviewed as independent streams.** [FIX + PARENT]
- *Evidence:* M5. The 24-verse fable at age 6 has 9 streams. After «الكل» is stamped, the pieces keep their own schedules.
- *Fix:* once «الكل» is memorised, retire the pieces' streams (or merge them into the whole). This reduces load and also rehearses the transitions between pieces, which piece-wise review never does.

**M-d. The thresholds for a recall take are permissive.** [PARENT]
- *Evidence:* `memOk` needs only ≥1.5 s per verse and loudness ≥0.08 (template.html:817). A classical verse takes 5–8 s to recite. The parent is the judge, so this is not a validity hole, but the child is told «غيباً! 🧠 …» before anyone has checked. That is premature praise for possibly partial recall.
- *Suggestion:* raise to ≈3 s per verse, and word the celebration «سجّلت محاولة غيباً، ينتظر أذن…» rather than «غيباً!».

**M-e. The pass rule effectively means 100% on short quizzes.** [FIX]
- *Evidence:* with 3 counted items, 2/3 = 67%; with 4, 3/4 = 75%; so 3–4 counted items need a perfect score, and 5 need 4. That is noisy in both directions: one slip fails a child who knows the poem.
- *Fix:* fix the length at 5 counted items, or use a lower bound (pass if correct − 1 ≥ 0.6n).

**M-f. Feedback timing is mixed.** [FIX, low cost]
- *Evidence:* most quizzes hold back the right answer until the end; that is defensible (delayed feedback is fine for retention). Order quizzes give immediate red flashes that allow trial-and-error elimination, which is what makes them guessable.
- *Fix:* in order quizzes, take the first tap per position as the answer (no retry), and show the correct sequence after.

### LOW

- **L-a. Ordered practice, no interleaving.** Reviews and quizzes are always one piece of one poem. A weekly mixed review would add desirable difficulty («أكمل البيت» across all memorised poems, or "which poem is this verse from?"). [PARENT]
- **L-b. Celebration inflation.** `cheer()` plays on every recording, including a level-1 read-aloud, and on practice quizzes ≥80%. The quiz-end display shows ⭐ per correct answer even when the run was practice. Keep the chime for effort, but reserve the big sheet for real milestones. [FIX]
- **L-c. 🧠 and the certificate are never revoked.** After repeated «ضعُف» the badge stays. A 🧠-with-🔁 state does exist (`due`). Acceptable, but consider showing «يحتاج تثبيتاً» after two consecutive «ضعُف». [PARENT]
- **L-d. Level progression (1→4) is free choice.** There is no nudge driven by success (for example, two clean takes at level 3 suggest level 4). The hint says «المرة القادمة: مستوى أصعب» only on the save sheet. [FIX]

## Q6. Motivation

Strengths, mostly intrinsic and social:
- The parent's voice as the model.
- «ردّد بعدي» as a turn-taking game.
- The family theatre.
- Before/after comparison, which is mastery evidence the child can hear.
- News from the parent, and recorded praise.
- The album sticker tied to parent-stamped 🧠. It is hard to game, and the right reward to attach a collectible to.

Risk:
- ⭐ is the gameable token **and** the path gate (C1/C2). A star-chasing 10-year-old will learn that tapping four quizzes a day is faster than memorising.
- The overjustification risk is moderate. Rewards are mostly informational («شارة جديدة», the parent's message), which is the less harmful kind.
- Once the gate moves to 🧠, ⭐ becomes a harmless practice token.

## Q7. Test suite as a specification

The tests (`tests/e2e*.py`, about 300 checks) specify UI rules well: practice labels, the first try per day, hiding answers until the end, stamps that wake after 70% heard, the review step moving on «ثابت», «ضعُف» returning tomorrow, and weak verses being counted. **Not specified, so free to regress:**
1. A take recorded when the piece is not due must not count as a review (H2). Nothing guards the interval against massed stamps.
2. ⭐ must come from the same piece or from «الكل» (H1).
3. Bounds on guessability: no test asserts that «أكمل البيت» has at least one same-poem distractor, that order windows are independent, or that easy order is practice for older children. The M1 strategies could become a regression test, for example "familiar+length < 50% per item".
4. `eok` should create a review schedule (H4), and the young path should eventually require delayed retrieval.
5. Behaviour of an unstamped review over time (H3): the due marker should not disappear for ever, and queue age should be shown.
6. A non-reader can finish a counted quiz with audio alone (H5).
7. Weak verses change practice beyond the order of «أكمل البيت» (M-a).
8. Review load: pieces retire when «الكل» is memorised (M-c).
9. No test checks that the first-day review of a just-memorised piece happens at the intended age-dependent interval (M-b, once decided).
10. No test covers lesson or talk engagement: nothing records it, so nothing can be tested.
