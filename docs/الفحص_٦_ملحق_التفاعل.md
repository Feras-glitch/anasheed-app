# أنشودتي: adversarial UX review (review 6)

Build reviewed: `www/index.html` built 2026-11-10 from `src/template.html`. I drove it in Chromium (Playwright) with a fake microphone at 390x844 and 360x740 (phones) and 820x1180 (tablet). I onboarded three children (دلال, girl, 10; فرح, boy, 8; كريم, boy, 6), recorded a parent model, ran Karim's echo session and a from-memory take, ran Dalal's quizzes, stamped takes on the parent page (including a stumbled verse), typed the «وصية لاجئ» pack from dummy key lines, opened «🎓 الدرس كاملاً», the picture story, the album, the archive and the backup export.
Screenshots are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/c9ec53c8-40a6-5db4-b7fa-8602a64d4718/scratchpad/review6/ux/` (written below as `ux/NN_*.png`).

There was no horizontal overflow at 360 px on home, poem, sing, parent, archive or album (I checked programmatically). There were no page errors; the only console error was the blocked Google Fonts request.

## Critical

### C1. On first launch the 6-year-old reaches a silent dead end. Nothing tells the parent that the app is mute until he records models.
- **Evidence:** `ux/01_first_launch.png`, `ux/10_karim_poem_open.png`, `ux/11_karim_story_full.png`, `ux/12_wait_model_tap.png`.
- **What happens:** On first launch the app goes straight to «أهلاً! من سينشد؟». It never explains the app to the parent. Karim's very first path step («الثعلب المغرور») opens in picture-story mode. Every picture carries 🦜⏳, and the only audio affordance reads «🦜⏳ ينتظر صوت بابا». Tapping it shows the same text as a toast.
- **Why it matters:** A non-reader has nothing to listen to, no TTS and no bundled recordings, so he cannot use the app at all. The parent finds out only by opening the parent page, where «مهمة الليلة» suggests recording 3 verses.
- **Fix:**
  - Add a one-screen parent setup on first run: your name, add the children, then "record the first model now (≈1 min)". Put it behind the gate, before the children's picker.
  - On the child side, make ⏳ show a big picture of the parent's face or icon plus a recorded or bundled jingle. It must not be text.
  - Best of all, ship at least one recited model per path-young poem so the first day works.

### C2. For a 6-year-old, progress depends entirely on the parent's ear, and the volume is not sustainable.
- **Evidence:** I computed these figures in-app with `segments()` and `pathOf()`:

  | Child | Path poems | Path segments | Segments across all poems |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | كريم (segment size 3) | 17 | 56 | 171 |
  | فرح | 17 | 40 | — |
  | دلال | 22 | 46 | — |

  Supporting screenshots: `ux/51_parent_queue.png`, `ux/52_queue_expanded.png`, `ux/54_after_stamp.png`.
- **What happens:**
  - A child of 7 or younger moves on a path step only when the parent stamps «✓ أتقنه» on an echo session for every segment (`stepDone`). A 3-verse echo session runs about 44 s.
  - Each from-memory take also needs a stamp, and each stamp schedules spaced reviews, each of which produces another take to hear.
  - Stamp buttons stay disabled until 70 % of the audio has played. That is good for honesty, but it multiplies time.
  - Models must be recorded per segment, and the segmentation differs per child (Karim's ١–٣ against Farah's ١–٤ on the same poem; `ux/113_queue_weak_chips.png`).
  - Rough load just for Karim's path: 56 model segments, about 56 echo approvals and 56 memorisation stamps, plus reviews. With three children that is several hundred listening acts.
- **Fix:**
  - Let one «✓» on a whole-poem take satisfy every segment of the echo requirement. This already exists for mem, so extend it to echoOk.
  - Allow "trust mode" for echo: auto-pass when the child completed all three rounds with voice activity in the turns.
  - Add batch listening: play all pending takes back to back and stamp with one big button per take, like a podcast queue.
  - Record models per poem, not per child-dependent segment. Today a parent recording under one child's segmentation is credited differently for another child.

## High

### H1. Echo mode («🦜 ردّد بعدي») throws the non-reader out of the picture story into dense text.
- **Evidence:** Compare `ux/30_karim_poem_with_model.png` (story mode with picture) with `ux/31_echo_1.png` and `ux/31_echo_3.png`. During echo the screen shows the level chips, the segment chips, ⚙️ and fully vowelled verses.
- **What happens:** `echoYoung` forces `mode='sing'`. The only cues are a dark/green bar labelled «👂 استمع» / «🗣️ دورك!» and transient toasts such as «١ · استمعي وردّدي» / «٢ · أكمل الشطر الثاني», which he cannot read.
- **Fix:**
  - Run echo inside the story view: the scene picture stays large and the current verse is highlighted under it.
  - Show the turn as a full-width icon: a big 👂 while listening, then a big 🗣️/🎤 pulsing.
  - Announce the rounds with a sound or a parent-recorded cue, not text.

### H2. Echo turn-taking runs on a fixed timer with no "I'm done" and no voice detection.
- **Evidence:** In the code, `turn(ms)` pauses for `(half × 1.3 + 0.9 s)` per hemistich and `(verse × 1.4 + 1.2 s)` for the whole verse. During the turn the bar shows only a progress line (`ux/31_echo_3.png`).
- **Why it matters:** A 6-year-old repeating classical Shawqi («مَن في النُهى مِثلِيَ حَتّى الوَرى») routinely needs more than 1.3 × the adult's time. The model then talks over him, which feels like failure.
- **Fix:** Use the VU meter that already exists, and extend the turn while the child is still voicing, up to a cap. Alternatively, add a big "✓ قلتُها" tap target. Default to more generous factors for age ≤ 7.

### H3. The 6-year-old's poem screen is a control panel, not a kid screen.
- **Evidence:** `ux/30_karim_poem_with_model.png`, `ux/40_karim_recording_lvl4.png`, `ux/72b_pack_poem.png`.
- **What it shows:** Up to 9 text buttons (قراءة / إنشاد / اختبر نفسك / حكاية مصورة / معنى البيت / حكاية النشيد / الدرس كاملاً / اسمع النموذج / ردّد بعدي / حديث مع والدك). Then «المقطع: ١–٣ ٤–٦ ٧–٨ الكل», «الحفظ: كامل نصف أوائل الكلمات غيباً 🧠 ⚙️», plus a hint line of text.
- **Problems:**
  - Modes (toggles) and one-shot sheets share one visual style.
  - In `ux/72b_pack_poem.png` two buttons are "on" at once («📖 قراءة» and «💡 معنى البيت»).
  - Segment chips are digit ranges, which mean nothing to a child who cannot read.
- **Fix:**
  - For age ≤ 7, show a separate minimal layout: picture plus three icon buttons (🔊 listen, 🦜 repeat, 🎤 sing). Show segments as picture thumbnails of their first scene, and hide the memorisation levels, ⚙️, quiz and lesson.
  - Keep the rich toolbar for 9+, but separate "mode" segmented control from "open sheet" actions.

### H4. Quizzes give a non-reader nothing he can do.
- **Evidence:** `ux/103_karim_quiz_menu.png`. Karim, on «🐣 سهل», sees «✍️ أكمل البيت (تدريب)», «💡 ما معنى الكلمة؟» and «🔢 رتّب الأبيات (تدريب)», all text-based. The audio quiz «🔊 اسمع واختر» did not appear even though a model existed: it needs at least 3 candidates, and one 3-verse model segment did not produce them.
- **Fix:**
  - For age ≤ 7, open the quiz tab directly on picture/audio games: "listen to the verse → tap its scene" from `STORY` images, which exist for every Shawqi fable.
  - If none is available, hide the quiz tab instead of showing text quizzes.

### H5. Toasts cover sheet titles and content again and again.
- **Evidence:** `ux/05_kid_switch.png` («أهلاً دلال» over «من سينشد الآن؟»), `ux/32_echo_done.png` (badge toast over «انتهى الترديد»), `ux/115_farah_news.png` (the stamp toast covers the news title «من والدك»), and `ux/71_after_save.png` / `ux/73_lesson_top.png` (the pack toast floats over the lesson body).
- **Fix:** When a sheet is open, put toasts at the top, or queue them until the sheet closes. Badge announcements already appear inside the celebration sheet, so drop the duplicate toast.

### H6. Gender agreement is incomplete in kid-facing labels.
- **Evidence:** `ux/62_dalal_quiz.png`. For Dalal, the header correctly says «اختبري نفسك» and the hint «المسي كلمة لتري معناها», but the quiz buttons (constant `QT`) stay masculine: «✍️ أكمل البيت», «🔊 اسمع واختر». The «ما معنى البيت» distractors are also masculine («فتعالَ نلعب فيه», `ux/68_quiz_sense.png`).
- **Further evidence from code:** Toasts «أوقف التسجيل أولاً», «اختر اسماً آخر», «اكتب اسم المنشد» and «توقّف التسجيل وحده…» are not passed through `gw()`.
- **Fix:** Route `QT` and every child-facing toast through `gw()`. Add a test that switches to a girl and greps the visible text for a list of masculine imperatives.

## Medium

### M1. The ⭐ symbol means two different things.
- **Evidence:** `ux/65_quiz_result.png` versus the legend on home.
- **What happens:** After a quiz, «⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ ٤ من ٥» counts correct answers. The ⭐ badge means "passed two different quiz types". The child gets 4 stars and is told «نجاحٌ في نوع آخر يمنحكِ ⭐» in the same screen.
- **Fix:** Show quiz scores as ✓ dots or a bar, and keep ⭐ only for the badge.

### M2. The motivation design is dense, and some of it is noise.
- **Evidence:** `ux/04_home_dalal.png`, `ux/06_home_karim.png`.
- **What the child sees:**
  - Four grey badges (👂🎤⭐🧠) repeated on every card and on the weekly banner.
  - A path card with 17–22 dots and «الخطوة ١ من ٢٢».
  - An album card «٠ من ٢٣» full of blurred lock tiles (`ux/101_album.png`).
  - A weekly challenge.
- **Why it's a problem:** The rules shown to the child are adult-grade («⭐ النجمة لمن ينجح في نوعين مختلفين من الاختبار · تُحسب أول محاولة لكل اختبار في اليوم»). For a 6-year-old the 22-dot strip is meaningless. A first-day album of 23 locks reads as "you have nothing".
- **Fix:**
  - For age ≤ 7, show the next 3 steps as pictures instead of dots.
  - Hide the album until the first sticker is earned.
  - Show badges only once earned (no grey placeholders).
  - Move the daily-first-attempt rule out of the child's view.

### M3. After a ✓ stamp, the parent dashboard still shows «حفظ غيباً ٠», so partial progress is invisible.
- **Evidence:** `ux/55_kpis_كريم.png`. I had just stamped ✓ حفظه on segment ١–٣, and the KPIs and badge table still show 0 and a grey 🧠, because poem-level 🧠 needs every segment.
- **Fix:** Show segment progress as "🧠 ١/٣ مقاطع" in the table and count memorised verses in the KPI.

### M4. The queue header and tonight's task contradict each other.
- **Evidence:** `ux/54_after_stamp.png`. After I stamped the only memorisation take, the header reads «📥 تنتظر أذنك (٠)», while «مهمة الليلة» says «١. اسمع تسجيلاً واحداً (في الأعلى)». The remaining item is the echo session hidden inside a collapsed «🦜 جلسات الترديد (١)».
- **Fix:** Count echo sessions in the header, or expand the echo group when it is the only pending item.

### M5. The path order contradicts its stated rule ("shortest first").
- **Evidence:** In-app data. Karim's path begins with «الثعلب المغرور» (8 verses), «الكلب والحمامة» (11), «الذئب والعظمة» (8) and «الثعلب والديك» (13). «طلع البدر علينا» (4 verses, the easiest and best known) is sixth. Dalal's path totals 297 verses.
- **Fix:** Re-order the young path by length and difficulty. Put «طلع البدر» and «بلادي بلادي» first so the first success comes on day one.

### M6. Age is the only lever for level, and it doesn't fit a child who has skipped a grade.
- **Evidence:** In-app data, cross-checked against the family profile. فرح is 8 but studies with دلال in grade 5. At age 8 he gets the 6–9 "young" path, segment size 4, and «لعمري (٨)» filters out the 9–12 school pack poems such as «وصية لاجئ» (age band [9,12]).
- **Workaround today:** Set his age to 10 in the parent page. That also changes segmentation and copy, which is a hack.
- **Fix:** Add a separate "level/grade" setting (or "show 9–12 poems") per child, independent of age-driven UI simplifications.

### M7. The kid-side save sheet has a free-text name field that silently creates new children.
- **Evidence:** `ux/41_karim_save_sheet.png`. The code does `if(!model && !kids.includes(who)){ kids.push(who) …; setKid(who) }`.
- **Why it matters:** A 6-year-old who taps the field and types anything creates a new child profile and switches to it. The audio control and the field are also noise for him.
- **Fix:**
  - On the kid side, replace the input with the kid-icon picker (or nothing), and keep the name field behind the gate.
  - Consider auto-saving the take with a big "🗑 أعد" undo, so the child never has to read «💾 احفظ».

### M8. The parent gate is weak, and it tells children how to defeat it.
- **Evidence:** `ux/20b_gate_pressing.png`. The gate is a 3-second press, and the sheet says «اضغط على الزر واستمر ٣ ثوانٍ». Dalal (10) and Farah (8) can read this.
- **Impact:** Behind the gate are stamping their own takes (self-awarding 🧠), deleting recordings, and editing models.
- **Fix:** Use a simple arithmetic or word question in written Arabic numerals (e.g. «٧ × ٨ ؟»), or a parent PIN, set during the parent setup in C1. Keep the long press as a first step if you like.

### M9. The lesson sheet («🎓 الدرس كاملاً») reads well but is 5 100 px long, with no close button at the top and no table of contents.
- **Evidence:** `ux/73_lesson_top.png`, `ux/75_lesson_scrolled.png`, `ux/76_lesson_scrolled2.png`; scrollHeight measured at 5 110 px at 390 px width.
- **What works:** Text size, line height, numbered lines and the collapsible ideas are good on a phone.
- **What doesn't:** The only exits are the thin backdrop strip, the Android back button, or «تمام» at the very bottom.
- **Fix:** Add a sticky header inside the sheet with ✕ and idea chips (١ ٢ ٣ ٤ ٥ 📘), and collapse "التحليل الفني" by default.

### M10. Typing a pack poem on a phone is a heavy, error-prone task.
- **Evidence:** `ux/70_parent_add_section.png`, and the pack instructions in `#packList`. For «وصية لاجئ» the parent is told: «٤٤ سطراً … في كل فكرة اكتب العمود الأيمن من أعلاه إلى أسفله ثم الأيسر، ثم السطرين في الوسط…».
- **Feedback after saving:** Only a toast and «(٤٤ بيتاً) 📦» (`ux/71_after_save.png`). There is no per-line check showing which typed line matched which pack key, or which were missing. The lesson later says «السطر ٣٠ لم يُكتب».
- **Fix:** After saving, show a match report («٤٣/٤٤ سطراً عُرفت؛ السطر ٣٠ ناقص») with tap-to-edit. Allow pasting from a photo/OCR, or typing line by line with the expected key word shown as a hint.

### M11. Jump links on the parent page land under the sticky index bar.
- **Evidence:** `ux/70_parent_add_section.png`, `ux/71_after_save.png`. After tapping «➕ أناشيد», the section title «➕ أناشيد إضافية» is hidden behind the sticky chip bar; only the first line of help text shows.
- **Fix:** Add `scroll-margin-top` on the `h2.sec` targets equal to header + index bar height (≈150 px).

### M12. Low-contrast «(تدريب)» labels sit on coloured quiz buttons.
- **Evidence:** `ux/62_dalal_quiz.png`, `ux/103_karim_quiz_menu.png`. «(تدريب)» is dark purple on purple and dark orange on orange, and nearly invisible. The question prompt «ما معنى «يصدح»؟» is also tiny grey text (`ux/66_quiz_meaning.png`).
- **Fix:** Use a white pill with dark text for the tag, and make the question prompt at least 18 px.

## Low

- **L1. The timer shows «٠:٠٠», which reads as «·:··».** Arabic-Indic zero is a dot, so the idle timer looks like noise to a child (`ux/30_karim_poem_with_model.png`). Hide the timer when idle, or show it only while recording.
- **L2. The archive shows children parent-only buttons.** ☆ (model) and a big red 🗑 sit on every row (`ux/50_archive_karim.png`). They are gated, but they invite taps that lead to the gate text. Show them only after the gate.
- **L3. The archive metadata line is cluttered with the year.** It reads «كريم · ١٠ نوفمبر ٢٠٢٦ · ٠:٠٦ · غيباً», and with the duration repeated on the play button it is busy. Drop the year for the current year.
- **L4. Story mode and the segment chips disagree.** In story mode the segment chips (١–٣ highlighted) suggest that only 3 verses are shown, but all 8 verses and 4 scenes are displayed (`ux/11_karim_story_full.png`).
- **L5. The gender/age screen allows age to be picked before gender.** Tapping an age closes the sheet immediately, so gender is silently skipped and the copy then defaults to masculine (`ux/03_age_gender.png`). Require gender first, or keep the sheet open until both are chosen. The control label «العمر و👦/👧» on the parent page is also awkward.
- **L6. Fonts depend on the build environment.** The build said «fonts: not found → Google Fonts link». In an offline app this means fallback fonts unless the release build bundles Amiri/Cairo. Verify that the dist bundle contains the font files.
- **L7. In model-recording mode only about one verse is visible at 390×844 while recording.** The header, mode banner, segment and level chips take about 620 px (`ux/23_model_recording.png`). Collapse the header while recording.
- **L8. The weekly-poem badges are greyed and unexplained** before any activity (`ux/04_home_dalal.png`). The explanation exists only as a small legend at the very bottom of the home list.

## What works well (briefly)
- **Clear tonight's task.** «مهمة الليلة» on the parent page is concrete, time-boxed («نحو ١ دقيقة») and has a one-tap «ابدأ» into the model session (`ux/21_parent_top.png`).
- **Honest stamping.** Stamps unlock only after 70 % listening; the parent taps the stumbled verse; weak verses feed «أكمل البيت» and leave after a clean take (`ux/113_queue_weak_chips.png`, «أبيات يتعثّر فيها فرح»).
- **Good feedback loop.** The parent's stamp comes back to the child as a «💌 من والدك» news sheet on home (`ux/115_farah_news.png`), and celebrations are personal («ما شاء الله يا كريم!», `ux/42_karim_celebrate.png`).
- **Kid-friendly post-echo screen.** It offers three big icon-only buttons (🎤 🔁 ⬅️), a good pattern that should be used everywhere for the youngest (`ux/32_echo_done.png`).
- **Strong picture stories.** The story illustrations are high quality and consistent (`ux/11_karim_story_full.png`, `ux/91_tablet_karim_story.png`). Animal icons (🐢 🐦 🦁) help non-readers find themselves.
- **Clean layout and resilience.** No horizontal overflow at 360 px. RTL is correct throughout. The tablet grid uses 3 columns sensibly (`ux/90_tablet_home.png`). The backup nag is prominent and the export explains the recordings option.
- **Pack lessons.** They are accurate and well structured, and lines the parent left out are named rather than invented.

## Score: 6/10 for the interaction
- **Strong for the 10-year-old and for a committed parent:** a rich, careful pedagogy (spaced reviews, weak verses, honest stamps, lessons) with attention to edge cases.
- **The weaknesses hit the app's stated hardest user:**
  - The 6-year-old non-reader opens it to silence (C1).
  - Echo strips away his pictures and runs on a timer (H1, H2).
  - He faces a text toolbar and text quizzes (H3, H4).
  - His progress is gated on a parent listening load in the hundreds of acts (C2).
- **Smaller cracks** add to it: overlapping toasts, incomplete gender agreement, the double-meaning ⭐, and badge/album noise.
- **Path to 8/10:** fix C1, C2 and H1–H4 with a dedicated ≤7 layout.
